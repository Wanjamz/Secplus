As a business grows, compliance tasks multiply. A simple checklist helps you see what recurs, where your documents are and when to ask for help.

Recurring corporate obligations

  • Annual return filed each year
  • Beneficial ownership information kept current
  • Statutory registers updated whenever changes occur
  • Changes in directors, shareholders or registered office notified to the Registrar
  • Board and shareholder decisions recorded in minutes or resolutions

Other recurring obligations to coordinate

Corporate filings sit alongside other obligations that may need different specialists, including tax returns and statutory deductions, county business permits, sector licences and, where applicable, registration with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Document organization

  • Certificate of incorporation and constitution
  • Statutory registers and minute books
  • Resolutions and filing confirmations
  • Share certificates and transfer records
  • Secure digital copies with controlled access

When to seek specialist review

  • Filings are overdue or you are unsure what has been filed
  • Ownership is changing or you are raising investment
  • You are preparing for due diligence, a tender or a bank facility
  • Records are incomplete after staff or adviser changes
  • You have received correspondence from a regulator

Official sources to check

  • Business Registration Service (BRS)
  • Kenya Revenue Authority
  • Office of the Data Protection Commissioner

This guide provides general information about Kenyan requirements as understood at the date of its last review. It is not legal advice. Requirements and fees change, so confirm the current position with the official sources above or speak to a qualified adviser about your circumstances.
