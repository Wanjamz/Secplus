If you are dealing with this issue, begin by understanding the applicable requirements and what information you will need. The correct approach depends on your entity and circumstances.

Foreign companies entering Kenya commonly choose between incorporating a local subsidiary and registering a branch. Each has different legal, governance and filing consequences.

Subsidiary

A subsidiary is a new Kenyan company, incorporated under the Companies Act, 2015 and owned wholly or partly by the foreign parent. It is a separate legal entity, so its liabilities are generally its own. It has its own directors, constitution and statutory obligations.

Branch (foreign company)

A branch is the Kenyan presence of the foreign company itself. The overseas company registers as a foreign company in Kenya after establishing a place of business here. A branch is not a separate legal entity, so the parent company is responsible for its obligations.

A registered foreign company must appoint at least one authorized representative resident in Kenya and must file certain documents with the Registrar, including information about the parent company and its accounts.

Governance differences

  • A subsidiary’s board takes decisions locally, subject to its constitution and shareholder approvals.
  • A branch acts on the authority of the parent company’s board and its local representative.
  • A subsidiary maintains Kenyan statutory registers; a branch’s filings focus on the parent company’s information and changes.

Filing considerations

Both structures have continuing filing obligations with the Registrar of Companies, and both need to register for tax. Tax treatment can differ significantly between a branch and a subsidiary, so obtain tax advice before deciding.

Advice checklist

  • What activities will the Kenyan presence carry out?
  • Does the parent want liability ring-fenced in Kenya?
  • Do local partners or investors need to hold shares?
  • Are there sector-specific ownership or licensing rules?
  • What are the tax implications of each option?
  • Who will act locally as director or authorized representative?

Official sources to check

  • Business Registration Service (BRS)
  • Kenya Law (Companies Act, 2015 and related regulations)
  • Kenya Revenue Authority
  • Kenya Investment Authority
