If you are dealing with this issue, begin by understanding the applicable requirements and what information you will need. The correct approach depends on your entity and circumstances.

Kenya’s company registration process is largely online, which makes it possible for many non-resident founders to establish a company without relocating. There are, however, some important questions to answer first.

Ownership questions

Foreign nationals and foreign companies can generally own shares in and be directors of a Kenyan private company. Some sectors have local ownership requirements or specific licensing conditions, so check the rules for your proposed activity before registering.

Documentation

  • Passport copies and contact details for foreign directors and shareholders
  • Proof of address for each individual
  • For a corporate shareholder: its certificate of incorporation, constitution and a board resolution authorizing the investment
  • Details of the beneficial owners who ultimately own or control the company
  • Tax registration details for officers, where required

Documents signed or issued outside Kenya may need to be certified, notarized or legalized before they are accepted. Confirm the requirement for your documents early, as this can take time.

Remote coordination

Much of the process can be coordinated remotely, but some steps, such as identity verification, opening a bank account or certain licence applications, may require additional verification or attendance in person. Agree communication channels and a secure way to share documents with your local adviser at the start.

Approvals to consider

  • Sector licences or regulatory approvals for your proposed activities
  • Work permits for foreign nationals who will work in Kenya
  • Optional investment registration with the Kenya Investment Authority

Post-registration obligations

A Kenyan company must keep a registered office in Kenya, maintain statutory registers, file annual returns, keep beneficial ownership information current and meet its tax obligations. Plan for local administrative support before you register.

Official sources to check

  • Business Registration Service (BRS)
  • Kenya Law (Companies Act, 2015 and related regulations)
  • Kenya Investment Authority
  • Department of Immigration Services
  • Kenya Revenue Authority

This guide provides general information about Kenyan requirements as understood at the date of its last review. It is not legal advice. Requirements and fees change, so confirm the current position with the official sources above or speak to a qualified adviser about your circumstances.
