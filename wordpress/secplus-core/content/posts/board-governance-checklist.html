Good governance depends on consistent, well-documented processes. Use this checklist to review your board’s current practice. Listed companies and regulated entities also have sector-specific governance requirements.

Meeting preparation

  • An annual board calendar covering meetings, approvals and filings
  • Agendas agreed with the chair in advance
  • Board papers circulated with enough time to read them
  • Quorum and notice requirements checked against the constitution
  • Conflicts of interest declared and recorded

Minutes

  • An accurate record of discussion, decisions and dissent
  • Action points with owners and deadlines
  • Minutes approved and signed at the next meeting
  • Minute books kept securely for the period required by law

Registers

  • Register of members
  • Register of directors and secretaries
  • Register of beneficial owners
  • Records of directors’ interests where applicable

Decisions

  • Written resolutions properly passed and filed
  • Clear delegated authorities for management
  • Decisions requiring shareholder approval identified

Policies

  • Board charter and committee terms of reference
  • Conflict of interest policy
  • Code of conduct
  • Risk management and whistleblowing arrangements

Review cadence

Review governance documents regularly and consider a periodic board evaluation. Listed and regulated companies should check the specific governance requirements of their regulator.

Official sources to check

  • Kenya Law (Companies Act, 2015 and related regulations)
  • Capital Markets Authority
  • The register of the professional body for Certified Secretaries in Kenya

This guide provides general information about Kenyan requirements as understood at the date of its last review. It is not legal advice. Requirements and fees change, so confirm the current position with the official sources above or speak to a qualified adviser about your circumstances.
