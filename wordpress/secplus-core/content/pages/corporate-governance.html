What does a governance review cover?

A governance review typically looks at board structure and composition, committee arrangements, policies, meeting processes and the quality of records. The scope is agreed with you at the start and depends on your organization’s needs and any sector requirements.

What is involved in board meeting support?

Board meeting support can include planning the annual board calendar, preparing agendas and notices, collating board papers, attending meetings, drafting minutes and tracking agreed actions.

Can SecPlus help prepare governance documents?

Yes. We can discuss board charters, committee terms of reference, conflict of interest policies and other governance documents suited to your organization.

What information is needed to scope an assignment?

Usually your constitution, current board and committee structure, existing policies, recent minutes and a description of what prompted the review. We will confirm what is needed during our initial discussion.
