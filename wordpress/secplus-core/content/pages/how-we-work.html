What happens at each step.

01

First contact

Submit a concise description of your company and requirement using our enquiry form. You do not need to send documents at this stage, and please do not send confidential identity or corporate documents through the form.

02

Initial discussion

SecPlus clarifies the scope, urgency, documents and the appropriate service. This is your opportunity to ask questions and understand the options that may apply.

03

Proposal

You receive a defined scope, assumptions, deliverables and commercial terms, so you know exactly what is included before you decide to proceed.

04

Engagement

Once you accept the proposal, we agree instructions and you provide documents through an appropriate secure channel.

05

Delivery and follow-up

You receive progress communication, the agreed outputs and an explanation of your continuing responsibilities. Ongoing support can be agreed where it is useful.

Getting started

What we need to begin.

A short description is enough for your first enquiry. It helps to include:

  • Your organization’s name, or your plans if it is not yet registered
  • Where you and the organization are based
  • The service or requirement you need help with
  • Any deadline you are working towards
  • How you prefer to be contacted

Handling confidential documents

Identity documents, registers and other corporate records are sensitive. We request them only after an engagement is agreed, and only through a secure channel agreed with you.

Information you provide is handled in line with our Privacy Policy.

View all FAQs

What information is needed initially?

A brief description of your organization and what you need. We may ask follow-up questions during our initial discussion.

Will I receive a proposal?

Yes. Where we can help, you will receive a proposal setting out the scope, assumptions, deliverables and commercial terms before any work begins.

How are confidential documents handled?

We request confidential documents only after an engagement is agreed, through an appropriate secure channel. Please do not send them through the website enquiry form.

How will I know who is responsible for my matter?

We introduce the professional responsible for your matter at the start of the engagement and explain how to reach them.
