Who we are

A specialist partner for your corporate responsibilities.

SecPlus Consulting is a Kenyan consultancy specializing in corporate secretarial, compliance and governance services. We support organizations with the corporate requirements that come with establishing, operating and governing an entity in Kenya, from company formation and statutory filings to board administration and corporate change.

We work with Kenyan founders, SMEs and established companies, as well as international investors and professional advisers who need a dependable Kenyan partner. Our role is to make corporate requirements understandable and manageable, so our clients can focus on running their organizations.

The principles behind our work.

Corporate matters are often sensitive and always important. These principles guide how we work with every client.

Clarity

We explain requirements in plain language and define technical terms, so you understand what applies and why.

Integrity

We give honest guidance about what is required, what we can help with and where other advisers are needed.

Responsiveness

We communicate clearly about progress and next steps throughout an engagement.

Confidentiality

We treat corporate and personal information with care and agree secure channels for sensitive documents.

Professional responsibility

We take ownership of the work we agree to deliver and the standards it must meet.

Expertise

Focused on the requirements that keep organizations in order.

Our work spans the corporate lifecycle. When you engage SecPlus, we introduce the professional responsible for your matter and explain their role, so you always know who is overseeing your work.
