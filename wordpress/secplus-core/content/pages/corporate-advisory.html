Before you get in touch

What to prepare.

You do not need everything in place to make an enquiry. These details help us understand your needs and suggest the right next step.

  • Your organization’s details, or the proposed structure if it is not yet registered
  • Relevant corporate records you already hold, such as certificates, registers or resolutions
  • A short description of the change, filing or requirement you need help with
  • Any deadlines, regulator correspondence or dates you are working towards
  • Your preferred contact method and the best time to reach you

How we keep your matter clear and confidential

  • A defined scope, assumptions and deliverables before work starts
  • A clear explanation of what you are responsible for and what we handle
  • Confidential documents requested only through an agreed, secure channel

What details should I provide about a restructuring?

A summary of the current structure, the change you are planning, the parties involved, your target timing and any constraints. Your constitution and any shareholder agreement are also helpful.

Which advisers may need to be involved?

Depending on the change, advocates, tax advisers, auditors or valuers may need to be involved. We explain where this is likely and coordinate within the agreed scope.

What approvals or filings could apply?

Most changes need board and, in some cases, shareholder approval, updated registers and filings with the Registrar. Some transactions also need regulatory approval. The requirements depend on the specific change and your sector.
