Can I enquire from outside Kenya?

Yes. You can enquire online from anywhere. We will discuss your plans and the most practical way to communicate and share documents.

Can I establish a Kenyan company while outside Kenya?

In many cases, much of the company registration process can be coordinated remotely, although documents signed abroad may need to be certified or notarized. Whether this works for you depends on your structure and circumstances, which we can discuss.

Do I need to visit Kenya?

Not always. Some steps, such as opening a bank account or obtaining certain approvals, may require in-person attendance or additional verification. We will explain what applies to your plans.

Do I need a Kenyan address or representative?

Kenyan companies must have a registered office in Kenya, and foreign companies registering a branch must appoint a local authorized representative. Other requirements depend on your structure and sector.

Can foreign shareholders own a Kenyan company?

Foreign ownership of Kenyan companies is generally permitted, but some sectors have local ownership requirements or licensing conditions. These should be checked for your proposed activity before you register.

Will I need separate tax, immigration or banking advice?

Often, yes. These areas can require specialist advice. We will explain where other advisers are needed and coordinate within the agreed scope.
