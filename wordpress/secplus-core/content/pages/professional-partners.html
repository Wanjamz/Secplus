How are referrals handled?

Start with a short outline of the matter. We complete conflict checks, then discuss scope, roles and terms with you and, where appropriate, your client.

Can SecPlus coordinate with overseas advisers?

Yes. Coordination arrangements, communication channels and responsibilities are agreed at the start of each matter.

How are confidentiality and conflicts managed?

We carry out conflict checks before accepting instructions and agree how confidential information will be shared and stored. Please do not send sensitive client data until this has been agreed.
