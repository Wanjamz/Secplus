How can I check whether my company is up to date?

A statutory health check compares your company’s records and registers with what has been filed, and identifies any outstanding returns or unrecorded changes. Share your company details and we can discuss a suitable review.

What records should my company maintain?

Kenyan companies are generally required to keep statutory registers, such as registers of members, directors and beneficial owners, together with minutes and resolutions. The exact requirements depend on your company type.

Can overdue filings be reviewed?

Yes. We can review your position, explain the steps required to bring filings up to date and discuss any penalties that may apply. Acting early usually makes corrections simpler.

What happens when company details change?

Changes such as new directors, share transfers or a new registered office usually need to be approved in line with your constitution, recorded in your registers and notified to the Registrar within the period required by law.

Can I request ongoing support?

Ongoing compliance and company secretarial support can be discussed where it suits your organization. The scope is agreed with you in advance.
