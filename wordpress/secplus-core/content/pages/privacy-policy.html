This Privacy Policy explains how SecPlus Consulting (“SecPlus”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects personal data when you use this website or contact us. We are committed to handling personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya and its regulations.

Who we are

SecPlus Consulting is a corporate secretarial, compliance and governance consultancy based in Kenya. For any privacy question or request, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Personal data we collect

  • Enquiry information you submit through our forms: your name, email address, telephone number (optional), organization, location, the type of support you are interested in and your message.
  • Correspondence you send to us by email or telephone.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the website, such as server logs that may include your IP address and browser type.

Please do not send confidential identity documents or corporate records through the website. If such documents are needed for an engagement, we will agree a secure process with you.

Chat assistant

The chat assistant on this website runs in your browser and answers general questions from information we have prepared. We do not store what you type into it. If you choose to continue a conversation on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that conversation, and we handle the information you send us there as described in this policy.

How we use personal data

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your requirements
  • To prepare proposals and, where you engage us, to deliver the agreed services
  • To keep appropriate records of our communications
  • To protect the website against spam, misuse and security threats
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations

Lawful basis

We process personal data on the basis of your consent (for example, when you submit an enquiry), to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, for our legitimate interests in operating and protecting our business, and where required by law.

Sharing personal data

We do not sell personal data. We share it only where necessary: with service providers who host or support our website and email systems, with professional advisers or authorities where required to deliver a service you have engaged us for, or where the law requires us to do so. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place as required by the Data Protection Act, 2019.

How long we keep personal data

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond to you and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping. Information relating to client engagements is retained for the periods required by law and professional obligations.

Your rights

Subject to the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to be informed about the use of your personal data, to access it, to object to its processing, to request correction or deletion of false or misleading data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, contact us. You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No internet transmission is completely secure, which is why we ask you not to send confidential documents through the website.

Cookies

Information about cookies is set out in our Cookie Notice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.
