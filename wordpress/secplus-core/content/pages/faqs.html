Company registration

What entity types can SecPlus help establish?

SecPlus can discuss business name registration, private and public companies, companies limited by guarantee and foreign company registration, as well as entity conversions.

What documents should I prepare?

Usually identification and contact details for proposed directors and shareholders, proposed names, intended activities and a registered office address. Additional documents may apply to foreign or corporate shareholders. We confirm the exact list for your situation.

What determines the cost and timeline?

The entity type, the number and location of directors and shareholders, how quickly documents are available and the processing times of the relevant authorities. Your proposal will explain what applies.

What obligations continue after registration?

Companies must maintain statutory registers, file annual returns, keep beneficial ownership information current and notify certain changes. Tax and licensing obligations may also apply.

International clients

Can I enquire from outside Kenya?

Yes. You can enquire online from anywhere, and we will agree a practical way to communicate and share documents.

Do I need a Kenyan address or representative?

Kenyan companies must have a registered office in Kenya, and a foreign company registering a branch must appoint a local authorized representative. Other requirements depend on your structure and sector.

Can foreign shareholders own a Kenyan company?

Foreign ownership is generally permitted, but some sectors have local ownership requirements or licensing conditions that should be checked for your proposed activity.

Will I need separate tax, immigration or banking advice?

Often, yes. We explain where specialist advisers are needed and coordinate within the agreed scope.

Compliance

How can I check whether my company is up to date?

A statutory health check compares your records and registers with your filings and identifies anything outstanding.

What records should my company maintain?

Kenyan companies are generally required to keep statutory registers, such as registers of members, directors and beneficial owners, together with minutes and resolutions.

Can overdue filings be reviewed?

Yes. We can review your position, explain the steps to bring filings up to date and discuss any penalties that may apply.

Can I request ongoing support?

Ongoing compliance and company secretarial support can be discussed and scoped to suit your organization.

Governance

What does a company secretary do?

A company secretary maintains statutory registers, prepares filings, supports board and shareholder meetings, keeps minutes and resolutions, and helps directors follow proper procedure.

What is involved in board meeting support?

Planning the annual calendar, preparing agendas and notices, collating board papers, attending meetings, drafting minutes and tracking actions.

How do governance reviews work?

We agree the scope, review your structures, policies, processes and records, and report our observations and suggested improvements.

Can SecPlus help prepare governance documents?

Yes. We can discuss board charters, committee terms of reference, conflict of interest policies and other governance documents.
