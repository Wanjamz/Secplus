Does my entity need a company secretary?

Under the Companies Act, 2015, public companies must have a qualified company secretary, and private companies above a specified paid-up capital threshold must also appoint one. Many smaller companies choose secretarial support anyway. We can discuss what applies to your company.

What does a company secretary do?

A company secretary typically maintains the statutory registers, prepares and files returns, supports board and shareholder meetings, keeps minutes and resolutions, and helps directors follow proper procedure.

What can be outsourced?

Most company secretarial tasks can be supported externally, including register maintenance, filings, meeting administration and resolutions. The board remains responsible for its decisions, and responsibilities are agreed clearly at the start.

How does ongoing support work?

Ongoing support is usually organized around your company’s calendar of meetings and filings. The scope, reporting arrangements and terms are agreed in advance.
