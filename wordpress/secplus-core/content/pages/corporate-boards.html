What does an outsourced company secretary do?

An outsourced company secretary maintains statutory registers, prepares filings, supports board and shareholder meetings, keeps minutes and resolutions, and helps directors follow proper procedure, as agreed in the engagement.

Can support be retained on an ongoing basis?

Ongoing support can be discussed and is usually organized around your governance calendar. Scope, reporting and terms are agreed in advance.

What documents are needed for a governance review?

Typically your constitution, board and committee structure, governance policies, recent board papers and minutes, and a short description of what prompted the review. We confirm the list when scoping.

How are board meetings supported?

Support can include the annual meeting calendar, agendas and notices, collating board papers, attending meetings, drafting minutes and tracking actions.
